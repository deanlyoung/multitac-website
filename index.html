TIQ vs. TAC Views

TIQ (Thought-Inspiring Question)

AI-led question-based workflow that guides you from problem to solution. Ask for specific outcomes or explore exploratory paths.

Gathers thoughts, values, and constraints → proposes visible TAC source

TAC (Thought-Activated Calculation)

Freeform text and calculations sharing one editor. Results appear beside or below the editor. Edit a card → dependents update automatically.

Core editor with live results, card pinning, and dependency tracking
