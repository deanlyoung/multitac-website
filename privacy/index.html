Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Overview

Multitac ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Multitac app and website.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We do not collect personally identifiable information when you use Multitac. The app is designed with privacy in mind, and all calculations and TIQ workflows occur locally on your device.

Usage Data

We may collect anonymous usage data to help us improve the app, including:

  • Feature usage statistics (anonymous)
  • Crash reports (anonymous, no personal data)
  • Performance metrics

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate and improve the Multitac app
  • Analyze how the app is being used
  • Detect and fix bugs and technical issues

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

All calculation data, TAC documents, and TIQ information are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your personal calculations or documents to our servers unless you explicitly choose to share them.

Third-Party Services

Multitac may use third-party services for:

  • App Store analytics (Apple Analytics)
  • Crash reporting (anonymous)

These third parties have their own privacy policies governing the data they collect.

Children's Privacy

Multitac does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will use commercially reasonable efforts to delete that information.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information
  • Object to processing of your information
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@multitac.app

Contact Us

If you have questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@multitac.app
