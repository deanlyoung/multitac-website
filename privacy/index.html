Privacy

Your work stays yours.

Multitac is designed around local documents, on-device calculation, and visible user control.

Last updated October 13, 2026 · Previous version

Summary

Multitac does not require a Multitac account or upload your notebooks to a Multitac server. Calculations run on your device. Memory is stored locally. When on-device Insights are available, notebook excerpts are processed by Apple’s on-device model without a cloud AI fallback.

Documents

Your TAC documents are files you create, open, and save. They remain under your control. If you save a document in iCloud Drive, Apple syncs that file according to your Apple Account and iCloud settings. Multitac does not operate a separate document sync service.

Memory

Memory Review proposes useful thoughts and calculated Results from TACs opened on the current device. Pending candidates record their TAC and source line but cannot influence Memory search, TIQs, or Insights until you approve them.

  • Memory does not crawl unrelated files or unopened iCloud documents.
  • Equivalent candidates may be grouped while retaining their contributing source references.
  • You can edit proposed wording, approve or dismiss candidates, exclude a TAC, forget approved items, pause learning, or clear all local Memory.
  • The local Memory Review and approved Memory index are not embedded in your TAC documents or synced by Multitac.

Insights and TIQs

Where supported, Multitac uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Model for reviewed, open-ended TIQ proposals and optional Insights. Guided, curated, and Personalization TIQs do not use a language model. The model receives a bounded selection of source text and calculated results. Multitac validates returned source references before display, but a valid reference does not prove the generated interpretation is correct.

Model-driven TIQ history and pending proposals may be stored in the TAC document. Kept and permanently dismissed Insight records are stored locally in app preferences. Other unkept suggestions are temporary. Multitac does not send this material to an external AI provider and has no cloud AI fallback.

Local app records

Before the first model-driven TIQ or Insight, Multitac shows an AI limitations notice. If you continue, the app stores a local receipt containing the notice version, acknowledgement date, app version, build number, and a digest of the notice text. It does not contain your name, email, Apple ID, device identifier, IP address, advertising identifier, or a generated fingerprint. Clearing app preferences or reinstalling the app may remove the receipt and cause the notice to appear again.

You can remove Memory through its controls, remove kept or hidden Insight history through the app, and remove TIQ history by editing or deleting the relevant TAC. Deleting app preferences or the app removes locally stored preferences, subject to operating-system backup and restoration behavior. TAC files remain wherever you chose to store them until you delete them there.

Diagnostics and analytics

The Multitac app does not operate its own analytics, advertising, or tracking system. Apple may provide App Store, crash, or diagnostic information under your device settings and Apple’s privacy policies. Any future telemetry added to the app will be described here before collection begins.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical request information such as your IP address, browser details, and requested URL to deliver and secure the site.

This website uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate site use, such as which pages are visited and the general types of devices and browsers used. Google may receive information about your visit and use cookies or similar technologies as part of this service. Multitac does not use Google Analytics to collect TAC content or serve advertising.

Data sharing and sale

Multitac does not sell personal information. It does not share TAC content with advertisers or data brokers.

Children

Multitac is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information through a Multitac account or service because no such account or service exists.

Changes

This policy may change as Multitac approaches release or gains features. The date above will identify the latest version. Material changes to data handling will be described before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@multitac.app.
